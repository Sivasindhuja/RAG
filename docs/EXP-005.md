# EXP-005 — Clause-Body-Aware Answer-Unit Selection

Status: **Rejected.** The production default remains EXP-003 (sentence units +
sibling-clause selection, `sibling_score_ratio=0.5`).

Last updated: 2026-11-14.

Companion documents: `docs/PRODUCTION_SLICE.md`, `docs/EXP-003.md`,
`docs/ENGINEERING_LOG.md`, `docs/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`.

---

## 1. Objective

Investigate why the accepted answer-selection pipeline fails to select the
required evidence for Q10 when that evidence is located inside the body of
clause 3.6.8, and determine whether clause-body-aware answer-unit segmentation
improves Q10 without degrading other questions.

## 2. Frozen pipeline

Unchanged:

- `data/satcom-ngp.pdf`
- approved 15 questions (`experiments/exp001_questions.csv`)
- 20 ground-truth evidence units (`experiments/exp001_ground_truth.json`)
- fixed-character 1000/200 chunking (49 chunks, production index reused)
- MiniLM `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`
- dense top-5 retrieval
- identical precomputed retrieval hits for both arms
- EXP-003 sibling-clause selection (`sibling_score_ratio=0.5`)
- citation construction, answer caps, abstention policy, metrics

No LLM generator was introduced. The accepted extractive production path was
used.

## 3. Failure analysis (before implementation)

### 3.1 Question and required evidence

- **Q10:** “What is the procedure when multiple applicants request the same
  orbit-spectrum in new or unplanned bands?”
- **Expected answer:** first come, first served when other conditions are
  fulfilled, with priority to government-owned systems in case of conflict;
  discussions between non-governmental applicants and the Administration to
  resolve conflicts; if unresolved, a common API is filed and the decision is
  taken at the AP S4 filing stage or later, depending on progress.
- **Relevant clause:** `3.6.8`.
- **Evidence:** `EXP001-Q10-E01`, page 8, offsets 17802–18522, excerpt:
  “For 3.6.7(a) and 3.6.7(b)... first come first served basis... Government
  owned system shall have priority... discussions with the Applicants...
  common Advanced Publication of Information (API)... decision... at the stage of
  AP S4 filing... or later depending upon the progress of coordination.”

### 3.2 Retrieved chunks and baseline answer

Retrieval was correct: Recall@5 `1.0`, MRR@5 `0.3333`. `EXP001-Q10-E01` overlaps
hit 3, `satcom-ngp::page_8::chunk_0::off17796` (section `3.6.8`, distance
`1.0165`, 720 overlap chars). That chunk contains the complete 3.6.8 procedure
followed by the start of 3.6.9.

The EXP-003 baseline selected 0/3 supported statements and cited no supporting
chunk:

| Statement | Chunk | Text (summary) | Supported |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1] | `satcom-ngp::page_7::chunk_3::off17330` | 3.6.7(a)-(e) definitional list (new orbit-spectrum, unplanned bands, planned bands, allotted slots, coordinated bands) | no |
| [2] | `satcom-ngp::page_8::chunk_1::off18596` (3.6.10) | “In this case a service operating license is not adequate…” (3.6.9 continuation) | no |
| [2] | `satcom-ngp::page_8::chunk_1::off18596` (3.6.10) | “3.6.10 For 3.6.7(e) above...” ICC announces availability and invites applications | no |

### 3.3 Root cause

1. **Fragmented clause body.** `_sentence_spans` split the 3.6.8 chunk into
   sentence/paragraph units. Clause 3.6.8 became four separate units:
   - “3.6.8 For 3.6.7(a) and 3.6.7(b)... first come first served basis.” (carries
     clause ID)
   - “In case of a conflict, Government owned system shall have priority.”
   - “In case two or more non-governmental systems ask for the same orbit
     spectrum... discussions with the Applicants...”
   - “In case such a solution is not possible... common API... decision at AP S4
     filing...”
   The 3.6.9 clause start in the same chunk correctly terminated the 3.6.8 body.
2. **Lost clause identity/procedure context.** Only the first unit started with
   `3.6.8`; the three body procedure sentences had no clause ID and could not be
   treated as the 3.6.8 procedure by sibling/clause logic.
3. **Lexical mismatch.** The question uses “new or unplanned bands” and
   “orbit-spectrum”; the 3.6.7(a)-(e) list contains those terms directly.
   Clause 3.6.8 refers to those cases as “3.6.7(a) and 3.6.7(b)” and contains
   procedure terms (“first come first served”, “priority”, “discussions”,
   “common API”, “AP S4 filing”) that overlap less strongly with the question.
4. **Selection/ranking effect.** Baseline candidate scores (IDF-weighted lexical
   overlap, same query tokens): list `11.323`, 3.6.10 sentence `6.790`,
   3.6.9-tail “In this case...” `5.801`, 3.6.8 body sentence `3.414` (best 3.6.8
   sentence), 3.6.11 `3.375`, etc. The 3-sentence cap and pure lexical ranking
   therefore excluded all 3.6.8 units.
5. **Completeness constraint.** The required evidence is the complete 3.6.8
   procedure. Even if 3.6.8 sentences ranked well, four steps cannot be fully
   represented by three separate sentence statements.

## 4. Hypothesis

If numbered subclause bodies are segmented into clause-body-aware answer units
rather than independent sentence units, the selector can preserve clause
identity and complete procedural steps. Grouping a depth>=3 clause start (e.g.
`3.6.8`) with its contiguous body sentences until the next numbered clause start
in the same chunk should make the 3.6.8 procedure a single selectable, verbatim
unit.

This was not assumed to be sufficient by itself: selection/ranking still had to
prefer the resulting unit.

## 5. Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Keep sentence units (EXP-003 baseline) | Known to miss Q10. Control arm. |
| Group every numbered clause start with its body | Rejected during inspection. Depth-2 headings such as `4.3` and `4.4` can span broad introductions or precede child clauses; a quick check showed Q13 selection collapsing to `4.3`, `4.1`, `4.4` instead of `4.3.2-4.3.4`. |
| Group only depth>=3 subclauses (chosen) | Smallest clause-body-aware change. Preserves 3.6.8 as one unit, leaves depth-2 headings as sentence units, and avoids swallowing child clauses under section headings. |
| Split every sentence / finer units | Opposite of the observed need; would worsen completeness. Not tried. |
| Change ranking, reference resolution, or scoring | Out of scope for this segmentation-only experiment. Identified as a follow-up if segmentation is insufficient. |

## 6. Implementation

### 6.1 Pipeline change

`src/rag_core/pipeline.py`:

- Added `_answer_unit_spans(text, clause_body_units=False)`.
  - Default: returns sentence spans (existing behavior).
  - When enabled: if a sentence starts with `_CLAUSE_START_RE` and the clause ID
    has depth >= 3 (`len(id.split(".")) >= 3`), the unit includes contiguous
    following sentence spans until the next numbered clause start in the same
    chunk.
  - The returned unit is the stripped exact substring `text[start:end]` with
    offsets preserved; no source text is fabricated or normalized.
  - Depth-2 headings are not grouped.
- Added `GroundedPipeline(..., clause_body_units: bool = False)` and passed it
  into candidate construction in `_extractive_answer`.
- Default behavior is unchanged (`False`).

### 6.2 Regression tests

`tests/test_pipeline.py` added:

- `test_clause_body_units_group_depth3_clause_with_following_body_sentences`:
  depth-3 `3.6.8` grouped with its body, stopped before `3.6.9`, offsets exact.
- `test_clause_body_units_do_not_group_depth2_headings`: depth-2 `4.3` remains a
  separate unit and does not swallow `4.3.1`.

No existing test was changed.

## 7. Pre-registered acceptance checks

The candidate would be accepted only if all passed:

1. **Q10 complete fix:** `EXP001-Q10-E01` covered, >=1 supported statement, cites
   `satcom-ngp::page_8::chunk_0::off17796`, and answer contains all key procedure
   phrases: “first come first served”, “Government owned system”, “priority”,
   “discussions with the Applicants”, “common Advanced Publication of
   Information” (or API), “AP S4 filing”.
2. **No non-Q10 regression:** for every other question, supported statements,
   supported citations and `answer_supported` not lower than baseline.
3. **Aggregate non-regression:** supported answers >= 12, statement support rate
   >= 0.3333, citation support rate >= 0.4961 (delta >= -0.02), abstention 12/12
   unchanged.
4. **Integrity:** statements/citations are verbatim source spans with valid
   offsets; retrieval unchanged (identical precomputed hits).

A Q10 score increase alone was not sufficient.

## 8. Experiment design

- **Runner:** `experiments/exp005.py`

- **Baseline arm:** EXP-003 accepted selection (`sibling_score_ratio=0.5`,

## 10. Decision

**Rejected (DR-012, RA-011).**

Failed checks: `q10_evidence_covered`, `q10_supported_statement_present`,
`q10_cites_clause_chunk`, `q10_all_key_phrases_present`,
`no_non_q10_regressions`, `statement_support_not_lower`.

Clause-body segmentation alone is **not sufficient** for Q10. It forms the
required 3.6.8 procedure unit and preserves its completeness/identity, but the
current lexical selection does not rank that unit above the 3.6.7(a)/(b)
definitional list, the 3.6.10 reference clause, or a 3.6.9-tail sentence. The
question’s key condition terms (“new or unplanned bands”) appear in the list,
while 3.6.8 refers to them as “3.6.7(a) and 3.6.7(b)”.

The production default remains unchanged.

## 11. Consequences and next experiment

- **Do not adopt** depth>=3 clause-body units as the default selector input.
- **Q10 next step (controlled, retrieval fixed):** a ranking/selection rule that
  uses clause-reference context. Candidates include scoring answer units with
  referenced clause text (e.g. resolve “3.6.7(a)/(b)” to the corresponding
  condition text already present in retrieved chunks) or preferring units that
  explicitly answer “For 3.6.7(a) and 3.6.7(b)” when the question asks about the
  referenced cases. This must be pre-registered and evaluated for regressions.
- **Q04/Q12:** heading/list-definition answer units remain a separate issue.
- **Q05/Q08:** retrieval misses, not answer-unit segmentation.
- **Acceptance design:** distinguish evidence loss from statement-granularity
  changes; inspect per-question evidence coverage and content.
